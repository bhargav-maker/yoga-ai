The Problem Statement

The Hidden Dangers of Solitary Yoga

Solitary home practice lacks crucial real-time posture validation and creates injury risks.

⚠️ High Injury Rates

  • Over 60% of solitary practitioners sustain lower back, neck, or sacroiliac strain.
  • Cannot look into a mirror while balancing or twisting without risking cervical sprains.
  • Uncorrected errors become deeply ingrained faulty muscle memory.

💸 Financial Inaccessibility

  • Personal certified yoga instructors charge $50–$100/hr, unaffordable for most families.
  • Rural schools and community centers lack access to certified yoga coaches.
  • Over 70% of fitness beginners drop out within 4 weeks due to lack of feedback.

🔒 Privacy Violations & Cloud Lag

  • Commercial fitness apps stream private home camera feeds to remote cloud servers.
  • 300ms–800ms cloud network latency causes choppy, delayed posture corrections.
  • Fails completely in exhibition halls, basements, and rural venues without Wi-Fi.

📺 The Flaw of Static Videos

  • Recorded YouTube videos offer zero interactive correction or form validation.
  • Users unknowingly cheat poses (unjoined hands, dangling legs, hyperextended knees).
  • Gives a false illusion of accomplishment while posture causes orthopedic harm.

Mathematical Foundations

Mathematical & Biomechanical Calculations

Rigorous geometry and spatial variance algorithms driving live posture validation.

📐 Joint Angle Vector Formulation

  • Calculates 2D joint angle θ at vertex B formed by limbs BA and BC:
  • θ = arccos( (u • v) / (||u|| * ||v||) )
  • where u = A - B and v = C - B in normalized coordinates.
  • Evaluates elbow flexion, knee flexion, hip abduction, and shoulder elevation.

🌊 EMA Keypoint Jitter Smoothing

  • Eliminates high-frequency sensor noise via Exponential Moving Average:
  • S(t) = α * Y(t) + (1 - α) * S(t-1) with α = 0.65
  • Maintains ultra-smooth joint positions without perceptible lag.
  • Prevents false angle fluctuation during stationary balance holding.

⚖️ CoG Spatial Variance for Stillness

  • Quantifies postural stability across a 15-frame sliding window:
  • σ² = (1/N) * Σ ||CoG(i) - mean(CoG)||²
  • Threshold: If σ² > ε, user is wobbling; hold timer pauses immediately.
  • Only activates when person is detected AND actively performing pose.

📏 Scale & Distance Invariance

  • Normalizes coordinate space relative to practitioner's physical torso size:
  • Torso Length = ||Shoulders_Center - Hips_Center||
  • All distance gates (e.g. wrist proximity <15% torso) scale dynamically.
  • Guarantees identical performance whether user stands 1.5m or 3.5m away.
Asana Library

7 Supported Classical Asanas

Comprehensive kinematic specifications covering balance, flexibility, and core strength.

🌳 Tree Pose (Vrikshasana)

Wrists prayer <15% torso length; standing leg straight (>150°); folded foot elevated near knee off ground.

🪑 Chair Pose (Utkatasana)

Bilateral knees flexed parallel between 75° and 140°; arms extended overhead above shoulder plane.

⚔️ Warrior Pose (Virabhadrasana)

Asymmetric lunge: front knee bent (75°-130°), rear leg extended (>140°); torso centered over pelvis.

🐕 Downward Dog (Adho Mukha)

Inverted 'V' shape: hips apex at highest point; arms and hamstrings lengthened with soft micro-bend.

🐍 Cobra Pose (Bhujangasana)

Thoracic spine extension: chest lifted >20° from floor; soft elbows (avoid locked joints) to protect spine.

🤸 Shoulderstand (Sarvangasana)

Full body inversion: vertical trunk column; hips stacked squarely over shoulders with core engagement.

📐 Triangle Pose (Trikonasana)

Lateral spinal hinge: arms aligned in vertical 180° vector; leading knee protected against hyperextension.

🧘 Negative 'No Pose' Class

Filters out standing, walking, stretching, or seated adjustments so idle postures never earn score.

Clinical Safety

Injury Prevention: Hyperextension & Symmetry

Active orthopedic guards protect ligaments, joint capsules, and spinal alignment.

🛑 Hyperextension Protection

  • Models anatomical range-of-motion limits for hinge joints (knees and elbows).
  • Reverse-deflection angles (>177°) immediately flag as hyperextension risks.
  • Visual Alert: Failing joint turns bright pulsing red on the skeleton canvas.
  • Spoken Guidance: Voice cautions: 'Softly bend your knee — avoid locking the joint'.

📐 Relative Slope Symmetry

  • Computes vector slopes of shoulder line (Δy/Δx) versus hip line relative to ground.
  • Detects pelvic tilt, hip drop, and asymmetric shoulder hiking (>12° divergence).
  • Catches compensatory strain before it becomes chronic lower-back pain.
  • Spoken guidance instructs practitioner to square hips and level shoulders.

🗣️ Prioritized Spoken Voice Coach

  • Built with native browser SpeechSynthesis API (100% offline, zero server cost).
  • Kinematic Hierarchy: Corrects spinal curvature before elbow/wrist placement.
  • Anti-Overlap Cooldown: Enforces 4.5s cooldown so it never talks over itself.
  • Enables eyes-free yoga practice during balancing and inversion postures.

🩺 Posture Recovery & Physical Therapy

  • Scorecard generates targeted physical therapy tips based on detected faults.
  • Suggests hamstring stretches for tight Downward Dog, or calf mobility for Tree Pose.
  • Transforms casual practice into clinical, restorative rehabilitation.
