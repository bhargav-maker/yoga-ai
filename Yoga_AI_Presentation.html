The Challenge

Why AI Biomechanical Guidance is Essential

Unsupervised home wellness practice suffers from high injury rates and zero validation.

⚠️

1. Form & Injury Risk

  • Spine curvature and knee hyperextension (>178°) cause chronic ligament damage.
  • Practitioners cannot observe their own spinal or pelvic tilt while stretching.
  • Flawed kinetic posture is reinforced into unconscious muscle memory.
💸

2. Prohibitive Coaching

  • Certified 1-on-1 yoga masters cost $50-$100/session with rigid scheduling.
  • Static YouTube recordings offer zero real-time correction or hold tracking.
  • Over 70% of beginners abandon home yoga within 3 weeks due to lack of guidance.
🔒

3. Privacy & Cloud Lag

  • Commercial vision apps stream living room video to cloud servers (privacy risk).
  • Server roundtrip lag (300-500ms) causes choppy, frustrating feedback loops.
  • Users demand completely private, local webcam processing on their machines.
Engineering Pipeline

End-to-End Client-Side AI Pipeline

How sensor frames travel from webcam to sub-millisecond biomechanical guidance.

1

Video Ingestion & Hardware Acceleration

React-Webcam captures 640×480 frames at 60 FPS directly into GPU WebGL memory buffers.

2

MoveNet Lightning Keypoint Extraction

TensorFlow.js extracts 17 spatial landmarks (x, y, confidence) across joints and spine in real time.

3

Bounding Box Normalization & Trigonometry

Calculates scale-invariant 34D vectors and computes exact geometric angles (knees, hips, elbows).

4

Dual Verification: DNN Classifier + Biomechanics

Deep neural network classifies asanas while geometric checks validate safe physiological limits.

5

Dynamic HUD Overlay & Voice Synthesizer

Renders emerald skeleton, triggers spoken voice cues, and advances safe delta hold streaks.
