The Problem Statement

Why AI-Powered Yoga Assistance?

Traditional home yoga practice lacks critical real-time posture validation.

⚠️

Risk of Injury

  • Incorrect joint angles and spine curvature cause chronic strain.
  • Practitioners cannot look in a mirror while balancing or twisting.
  • Lack of posture feedback ingrains bad habits into muscle memory.
💸

Trainer Inaccessibility

  • Certified 1-on-1 yoga instructors are expensive and hard to schedule.
  • Static videos (YouTube) offer zero interactive correction.
  • Over 70% of fitness beginners drop out without guided accountability.
🔒

Privacy & Cloud Lag

  • Cloud-based computer vision services stream live video to external servers.
  • Cloud network latency (300ms+) causes choppy, delayed feedback.
  • Users demand complete camera privacy in home workout spaces.
The Solution

Introducing Yoga AI (Poddar Yogis)

A high-precision, 100% on-device AI system that coaches users in real time.

⚡

Real-Time MoveNet Keypoint Tracking

Extracts 17 skeletal landmarks (shoulders, elbows, knees, ankles) at a butter-smooth 30–60 FPS. Uses dynamic aspect-ratio canvas scaling to overlay the skeleton precisely on the user's body.

🛡️

100% Client-Side Privacy

All machine learning inference executes locally in the web browser using WebGL and TensorFlow.js. Zero video frames leave the device, ensuring total privacy and instantaneous feedback.

🧠

Custom Pose Classification DNN

A deep neural network evaluates normalized 34-dimensional feature vectors to verify if the user's posture matches targeted classical yoga asanas with high confidence thresholding.

⏱️

Hold-Streak Timer & Sound Cues

Gamified posture holding: the timer increments only while the correct pose is sustained, accompanied by audible milestone chimes and personal best records.

Engineering Pipeline

System Architecture

How frames travel from the webcam sensor to real-time feedback in milliseconds.

1

Live Video Ingestion

React-Webcam streams 640×480 video frames at 30+ FPS into an HTML5 memory buffer.

2

MoveNet Lightning Detection

TensorFlow.js extracts 17 spatial coordinates [x, y, confidence] across the full human body.

3

Bounding Box Normalization

Transforms raw pixel coordinates into scale-invariant 34-dimensional feature vectors (distance independent).

4

Custom Classifier Inference

Deep neural network computes class probabilities across 7 asanas with confidence thresholding (>0.85).

5

Interactive Skeleton & Audio Feedback

Renders real-time joint-to-joint lines on synchronized canvas; increments pose hold timer with audio chime.

Curated Poses

7 Supported Classical Asanas

Carefully selected to train balance, upper body, spine, and lower extremities.

🌳 Tree Pose (Vrikshasana)

Trains neuromuscular coordination, ankle stabilization, and hip opener balance.

🪑 Chair Pose (Utkatasana)

Strengthens quadriceps, glutes, spine alignment, and stimulates abdominal organs.

🐍 Cobra Pose (Bhujangasana)

Promotes deep thoracic spine flexibility, opens chest, and tones shoulder girdle.

⚔️ Warrior Pose (Virabhadrasana)

Develops leg stamina, core stability, focus, and hip flexor mobility.
